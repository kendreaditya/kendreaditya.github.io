Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy covers the personal gog CLI Google OAuth integration operated by Aditya Kendre. It is used only by Aditya Kendre to access his own Google Workspace data from his local computer.

Google data the integration accesses

The integration may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets, and Google Contacts when an authenticated command is run.

How data is used

Google data is used only to perform the command requested by the account owner, such as reading an email, listing calendar events, or accessing a document. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training models.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth credentials are stored locally in the account owner's macOS Keychain.
  • Google data is not sold or shared with third parties.
  • No Google data is stored on this website.

Security and retention

Access is protected by Google's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. Data is retained only when the account owner explicitly saves command output. OAuth access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to kendreaditya@gmail.com.

This is a private, single-user integration and is not offered as a public application or service.
